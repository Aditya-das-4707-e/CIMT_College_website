Our Stars

Recognizing the exceptional talents and accomplishments of our students

At Chandannagar Institute of Management & Technology, we take immense pride in the achievements of our students. Our performers and achievers represent the very best of our institution, showcasing excellence in academics, sports, cultural activities, and community service.
