Our Curriculum

Balanced academic structure combining theoretical knowledge with practical application

Industrial Training and Project

Our curriculum includes mandatory industrial training and project work to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world application. Students undertake internships with leading organizations, gaining hands-on experience in their field of study. The final year project allows students to apply their cumulative knowledge to solve practical problems under faculty guidance.

Seminar, Conference, Workshop

Regular seminars, conferences, and workshops form an integral part of our curriculum. These events provide platforms for students to interact with industry experts, learn about emerging trends, and develop presentation skills. Our annual tech fest and management conclave attract participation from institutions across the region, fostering healthy competition and knowledge exchange.
