Co-Curriculum

Enhancing learning through diverse activities beyond the classroom

Our Co-Curricular Activities

Activities that complement academic learning and foster holistic development

Activities We Offer

  • Sports & Athletics: Annual sports meet, inter-college competitions, and regular training sessions
  • Cultural Events: Annual fest, drama club, music competitions, and art exhibitions
  • Debate & Elocution: Regular debate competitions, public speaking workshops
  • Student Clubs: Entrepreneurship club, coding club, literary society
  • Community Service: NSS activities, blood donation camps, village adoption program
  • Tech Events: Hackathons, coding competitions, tech workshops
  • Guest Lectures: Industry experts and alumni interaction sessions
  • Field Trips: Industrial visits, educational tours, and cultural excursions

Benefits of Co-Curricular Activities

How these activities contribute to our students' overall development

Skill Development

Enhances leadership, teamwork, communication and problem-solving skills beyond academic learning.

Personality Growth

Builds confidence, emotional intelligence and helps discover hidden talents and interests.

Career Advantages

Provides practical experience and makes students more attractive to potential employers.

Work-Life Balance

Teaches time management and provides a healthy break from academic pressures.
