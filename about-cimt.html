History of C.I.M.T

Established in 2001, Chandannagar Institute of Management & Technology (CIMT) has been a pioneer in providing quality education in the fields of management and technology in the Hooghly district of West Bengal. Our institution was founded with the vision to create a center of excellence that bridges the gap between academic knowledge and industry requirements. With a rich legacy of 24 years in fostering world-class academic excellence and over 25000 alumni across the globe, CIMT is one of the premier institutions of higher learning in India offering Undergraduate programs.

Over the past two decades, CIMT has grown from a modest beginning to become one of the most respected institutions in the region. We take pride in our alumni who have excelled in various professional fields across India and abroad. Our campus, located in the heart of Chinsurah, has evolved to include state-of-the-art facilities while maintaining our commitment to affordable education. Uniquely positioned as one of the first affiliated management college under The University of BURDWAN that offers the highest standard of professional education ingrained with the postulates of management studies, CIMT enables an inclusive culture of dignity, fairness, and respect amongst its students, grooming them into future-ready, responsible citizens of the world.
