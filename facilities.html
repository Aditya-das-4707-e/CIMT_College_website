Our Facilities

State-of-the-art infrastructure to support quality education

Modern Learning Facilities

We provide world-class infrastructure to enhance the learning experience of our students

Computer Lab

Our fully-equipped computer lab features high-performance systems with the latest software for programming, data analysis, and multimedia applications. The lab provides students with hands-on experience in various technologies.

Hardware Lab

The hardware lab is equipped with modern tools and components for practical learning in computer hardware, networking, and embedded systems. Students gain practical skills in assembling, troubleshooting, and maintaining computer systems.

Projector Room

Our modern projector rooms facilitate interactive learning with high-quality audiovisual equipment. These spaces are used for presentations, seminars, and multimedia-based teaching to enhance student engagement.

Library

The college library houses an extensive collection of books, journals, and digital resources covering management and technology subjects. It provides a quiet study environment and research support for students and faculty.
